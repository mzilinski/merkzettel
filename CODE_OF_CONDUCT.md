# Code of Conduct

Merkzettel is built by volunteers. We want everyone who reports a bug,
translates a string or sends a patch to feel welcome.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people whose first
  language is not English or German.
- Assume good intent; ask before you conclude.
- Critique ideas and code, not people. Explain your reasoning.
- Accept that maintainers have to say "no" sometimes, and say it politely
  yourself.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies to the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions and any other
space run by the project, and to representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer at **malte@zilinski.eu**. Reports
are handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions,
and temporarily or permanently block participants who violate this code.
Actions taken are explained to the person concerned.
